# Candle Desk Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Candle Desk ("the app") is a paper-trading simulator. It does not use real money and does not place real trades. This policy explains what information the app handles.

## What stays on your device

Your paper accounts, balances, positions, trade history, prop firm accounts, watchlist, chart settings, drawings, price alerts, bot settings and notification choices are stored only on your device. We do not run a server for the app, we do not receive this data, and we cannot see it. If you uninstall the app or erase its data, it is deleted. Backups you create with "Backup and restore" are files that you save and manage yourself.

## What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

## Connections the app makes

To show prices, the app connects directly from your device to market-data services:

- **Coinbase Exchange** (api.exchange.coinbase.com) for live crypto prices and chart history.
- **OANDA** (api-fxtrade.oanda.com, stream-fxtrade.oanda.com and the matching practice servers), only if you choose to turn on forex prices and enter your own OANDA API token. The token is stored on your device and sent only to OANDA to fetch prices. You can remove it at any time.
- An optional economic-news calendar address, only if you enter one yourself under the prop firm settings.

These services receive the technical information any internet connection involves, such as your IP address, and are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control them.

## Purchases

Candle Desk Pro is sold through the Microsoft Store. Payment is handled entirely by Microsoft. The app only asks the Microsoft Store whether your Microsoft account owns Pro, and keeps a yes or no answer on your device so Pro still works offline. We do not receive your payment details or identity.

## Notifications

The app can show system notifications on your device (for fills, stops, price alerts and warnings). They are created locally by the app and are not sent through any server. You can turn them off or choose which kinds to receive in the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Not financial advice

Candle Desk is for education and practice only. Prices may be delayed or wrong, nothing in the app is investment advice, and results in the simulator do not predict real trading results. Prop firm rule sets are generic examples and are not affiliated with or endorsed by any firm.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
